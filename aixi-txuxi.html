Chapter 3 · AIXI-Shape & TXUXI

Controlled Validation on Synthetic Data

An exhaustive evaluation of TCAV and Visual-TCAV on synthetic benchmarks where the contribution of every concept to every label is known in closed form and the footprint of every concept is available as a per-pixel mask.

01

Why synthetic ground truth

How faithful is TCAV, and how spatially faithful is Visual-TCAV?

Two problems were left open by the review. Neither method admits a principled ground-truth validation when applied directly to dermoscopy, because the true sensitivity of a trained classifier to a clinically defined concept is unknown; qualitative inspection against domain knowledge is circular. And recent corroboration work has exposed pitfalls, from the choice of negative reference pool to the construction of the CAV, that can make a nominally identical pipeline trustworthy or misleading.

On AIXI-Shape and TXUXI, a faithful method should recover attributions proportional to the ground-truth contributions of the generating function, and concept maps that coincide with the known footprint of the concept. Two questions are studied: attribution correctness for TCAV and spatial fidelity for Visual-TCAV, while three factors vary independently.

Model complexity

A family of classifiers of increasing size is trained on the same task, showing whether reliability is intrinsic to the method or a by-product of network capacity.

Probing depth

The CAV and concept map are recomputed at each convolutional layer, tracing how concept-aligned representations build up through the network.

CAV construction

Every result derives from one CAV, so two estimators are compared under identical conditions: a geometric centroid method and a linear classifier.

02

AIXI-Shape and TXUXI

The generating process is fully deterministic, every concept instance has a per-pixel binary mask, and the textured variant stress-tests the methods under naturalistic backgrounds.

Each image is a $128 \times 128$ single-channel canvas on which up to three geometric primitives, circles, squares and crosses, are rendered at pseudo-random positions on a structured $3 \times 3$ grid. In AIXI the background is held at zero intensity and shapes at full intensity. TXUXI replaces the background with a natural texture from the Describable Textures Dataset while preserving the shape footprints.

The number of instances of each shape is drawn independently, $c, s, cr \in \{0, 1, 2\}$. The label is the result of an attribution function applied to that count triplet; in the first phase, exactly one shape carries unit evidential weight, so the ground-truth importance ordering is unambiguous. The masks are generated by the same procedure as the images, an exact spatial gold standard free from annotation noise.

03

FlexTNet

A configurable CNN used to control model capacity, parameterised by a channel sequence $[c_1, \dots, c_N]$ and a unit sequence $[d_1, \dots, d_M]$. Each convolutional block halves the spatial resolution, so an $N$-block network reduces the input to a $128/2^N$ feature map; the network ends in a single sigmoid unit.

04

CAV construction and pools

Activations of probing layer $\ell$ are denoted $f_\ell(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times H \times W}$, and the CAV $\hat{v}_\ell^c$ is a vector in this space associated with concept $c$. Three choices are compared: the construction method, the activation pooling, and the negative reference pool.

Under the classifier construction, a Support Vector Machine and a Logistic Regression are each fitted on an 80/20 split of the pooled concept and negative activations. The expected failures are sensitivity to influential observations and a tendency to fit dataset-shift axes rather than the concept axis when the two pools are distributionally distant. The negative pool is the variable both constructions are expected to be most sensitive to:

Out-of-Distribution

Natural photographs from ImageNet for AIXI and Describable Textures crops for the textured variant: real-world imagery structurally far from the shape-bearing positives.

Matched-Absent

Validation images from the same AIXI-Shape generating process, filtered to exclude every instance of the target shape: same distribution, guaranteed concept-absent.

Matched-Blind

Validation images from the same process without any label filter, in the style of the dataset-blind protocol of dermoscopic work. It inherits the natural base rate of every concept.

05

Evaluation metrics

Directional and attribution metrics summarise the sign and magnitude of a concept’s effect on the class logit for TCAV; spatial metrics compare the continuous Visual-TCAV map against the binary ground-truth mask.

Conceptual sensitivity

Let $h_\ell : \mathbb{R}^C \to \mathbb{R}$ be the remaining layers of the network, mapping the pooled activations $\bar{a}(x)$ to a class logit. The conceptual sensitivity is the directional derivative of this logit along the unit concept vector; a positive value means the concept pushes the model toward the target class:

$$ S_\ell^c(x) = \nabla h_\ell\big(\bar{a}(x)\big) \cdot \hat{v}_\ell^c $$

Sign-count score. The canonical TCAV score, the fraction of test images with a positive derivative. A score above 0.5 means the concept predominantly drives the model toward the class.

$$ \mathrm{TCAVQ}_C = \frac{\big|\{x \in \mathcal{X} : S_\ell^c(x) > 0\}\big|}{|\mathcal{X}|} $$

Mean signed magnitude. Retains both the sign and the magnitude of the derivative, distinguishing a consistently large influence from a consistently negligible one.

$$ \mathrm{MSM} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{X}|} \sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} S_\ell^c(x) $$

Polarity-correctness. A configuration evaluates the sign count of the target concept $c^\star$ and of the two distractors $c \neq c^\star$. It is polarity-correct if the target is above 0.5 while every distractor stays below it:

$$ \text{polarity-correct} \iff \mathrm{SC}^{c^\star} > 0.5 \;\wedge\; \max_{c \neq c^\star} \mathrm{SC}^{c} < 0.5 $$

Polarity-Correctness Rate. The fraction of configurations within a grouping $G$ that satisfy the criterion:

$$ \mathrm{PCR} = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} \mathbf{1}\Big[ \mathrm{SC}_g^{c^\star} > 0.5 \;\wedge\; \max_{c \neq c^\star} \mathrm{SC}_g^{c} < 0.5 \Big] $$

Spatial correctness

The Visual-TCAV map carries a reliable spatial ordering but no calibrated magnitude, so only metrics that compare a continuous map with a binary mask are used. For each location $(h, w)$ of the upsampled $128 \times 128$ grid, $\hat{M}(h, w)$ is the concept map value and $\mathrm{GT}(h, w) \in \{0, 1\}$ the ground-truth label. Spatial-AuC treats $\hat{M}$ as a per-pixel classifier and sweeps a threshold $\tau$:

$$ \text{Spatial-AuC} = \int_0^1 \mathrm{TPR}(\tau) \cdot \frac{d\,\mathrm{FPR}(\tau)}{d\tau}\, d\tau $$

It is magnitude-invariant: 0.5 is chance, 1.0 is perfect localisation, and below 0.5 the map is more active outside the shape than inside. The soft IoU extends the Jaccard index to the continuous map without binarisation, and is sensitive to absolute magnitude:

$$ \mathrm{IoU} = \frac{\sum_{h,w} \hat{M}(h,w)\,\mathrm{GT}(h,w)}{\sum_{h,w} \hat{M}(h,w) + \sum_{h,w} \mathrm{GT}(h,w) - \sum_{h,w} \hat{M}(h,w)\,\mathrm{GT}(h,w)} $$

Spatial-AuC evaluates whether the activation is concentrated in the right place; IoU evaluates whether it covers the right place in the right amount. The concept map is upsampled to $128 \times 128$ rather than downsampling the mask, which would make small shapes disappear.

06

Scalar attribution with TCAV

Nine experiments, each isolating one design variable. Both frameworks analyse sixteen FlexTNet checkpoints; CAVs are built with Centroid, regularised Logistic Regression and SVM against three pools at every convolutional layer. Visual-TCAV covers 528 configurations per dataset variant.

Experiment 1

Canonical TCAV and the negative pool

With the medium FlexTNet, the bottleneck $\ell_2$ and an SVM CAV fixed, only the negative pool varies. The circle model is the trivial case: target 1.00 and distractors 0.00 under every pool. The cross model returns three different verdicts from the same activations.

Under OOD every concept scores 1.00: with ImageNet negatives, every CAV converges onto the same AIXI-versus-ImageNet direction. Under matched-blind the target collapses to 0.00, as the natural rate of crosses in the negatives inverts the direction. Only matched-absent is correct, with the target at 1.00 and both distractors at 0.00. The pool, not the model or the layer, governs whether the test means anything.

Experiment 2

Pool dependence across the grid

The Polarity-Correctness Rate is computed over all sizes, layers and concepts. The construction barely matters: Centroid and Logistic Regression are near-identical in every group. The pool drives correctness: for the two stable constructions the OOD rate sits almost on the floor while the matched pools sit roughly ten times higher. Even the best pool clears only about a quarter of configurations overall.

Experiment 3

Where the concept signal lives

Resolved layer by layer, the rate under both matched pools stays low at $\ell_0$ and $\ell_1$, peaks at the bottleneck $\ell_2$ and collapses at $\ell_3$, as linear probing predicts. The rising OOD rate at depth is a small-sample distortion: only four configurations reach $\ell_3$, so one pass reads as 0.250. The genuine signal lives at the bottleneck.

Experiment 4

What the polarity test hides

The mean signed magnitude reveals two failures the polarity test concealed. Under OOD the target derivative becomes negative for the cross and the square, on the same side of zero as the distractors. Under matched-blind the cross drives both target and distractors deep into negative territory, a meaningless “pass”. Matched-absent is the only pool that recovers both the correct sign and the expected ordering.

Experiment 5

Construction method and pooling space

Per-image sign agreement explains the SVM’s apparent lift under OOD. Bulk-driven methods (Centroid and Logistic Regression) lock onto the same dataset-shift anomaly and agree almost totally on the wrong feature; the boundary-driven SVM becomes unstable and its agreement with the Centroid drops to 0.500. Under matched-absent the SVM agrees with the Centroid roughly 90% of the time, so the choice of algorithm is immaterial and the parameter-free Centroid is recommended.

In the flattened space the SVM fails: thousands of dimensions fitted on a few dozen exemplars, a $p \gg n$ regime, give an inflated held-out accuracy along a direction that also fires on the cross. GAP is preferred because it is cheaper by a factor of $HW$ and removes this instability.

07

Spatial fidelity with Visual-TCAV

Visual-TCAV reuses the same CAV to form a concept map, which AIXI and TXUXI let us compare pixel for pixel against the ground-truth mask.

Experiment 6

The same pool dependence

Every configuration above 0.85 shares a matched or texture-aligned negative pool, and the single catastrophic failure, Centroid against OOD on the plain variant, is the one case where the negatives are structurally distant from the positives. Under matched-absent the Centroid and the Classifier are indistinguishable; the Centroid is simply more sensitive to a bad pool.

On TXUXI the OOD reference is itself drawn from the DTD backgrounds, so it is barely out of distribution, and the Centroid reaches its highest score there (0.976). The failure is a property of distributional distance, not of the construction.

Experiment 7

Best depth and the calibration gap

The bottleneck is the best layer for the majority of configurations, with Spatial-AuC climbing into $[0.96, 1.00]$ for every model of sufficient capacity, while the IoU at those configurations stays in $[0.15, 0.38]$. The gap is a property of the map, not of the measurement: its values are the concept’s strength, not a shape mask, so Visual-TCAV puts its activation in the right place but spreads it wider than the shape.

Experiment 8

Against the saliency baselines

On the textured TXUXI benchmark, the Classifier-based Visual-TCAV achieves the highest Spatial-AuC alongside Integrated Gradients, while the other baselines fall significantly behind. What separates it from its nearest rivals is specificity: the lowest off-target activation, avoiding the false-positive penalties of LIME and the computational cost of Occlusion.

Grad-CAM’s collapse is a resolution effect: its map is read from as small as 4 × 4 pixels, and upsampling creates receptive-field misalignment. The baselines answer which pixels move a single prediction, whereas Visual-TCAV answers which regions map onto a predefined concept.

08

Cross-method convergence

Experiment 9: do the scalar score and the spatial map agree on which concepts drive the model?

They agree. Over every (shape, layer, concept) configuration of AIXI, each axis split at its decision threshold (0.5 for the sign-count, 1/3 for the three-concept attribution fraction), the targets fall in the upper right and the distractors in the lower left. The two place the concept on the same side of their threshold in 28 of the 39 configurations, and the scores correlate at Pearson $r = 0.70$ and Spearman $\rho = 0.77$.

Because the methods are not equivalent by construction, this agreement licenses each as an independent check: the importance ordering is a property of the model itself.

09

Conclusions

The nine experiments support four claims about the reliability of TCAV and Visual-TCAV on this benchmark.

  • The negative pool governs faithfulness. Within a fixed pool the two stable constructions differ by at most 0.074 in aggregate polarity and the SVM by up to 0.18, while the OOD rate collapses to 0.02 to 0.05. An OOD pool aligns the CAV with the dataset-shift axis; a dataset-blind pool lets the base rate of the target contaminate the negatives and invert the direction. Only the matched-absent pool gives the expected sign and ordering.
  • The signal lives at the bottleneck. The polarity-correctness rate peaks at $\ell_2$, reaching 0.500 under both matched pools, decays to near zero at shallower layers and to exactly zero at $\ell_3$, whichever construction fits the direction.
  • Visual-TCAV localises, positionally. With a matched pool, Spatial-AuC lies in $[0.96, 1.00]$ at the optimal layer of every architecture of sufficient capacity; the Centroid OOD failure recovers from 0.227 to 0.901 under matched-absent. The same configurations attain an IoU below 0.35.
  • Top of the field on TXUXI. The Classifier-based Visual-TCAV reaches a Spatial-AuC of 0.967, level with Integrated Gradients at 0.965, with the lowest off-target activation (0.005), while Grad-CAM falls to 0.592.
