Overview

Project abstract

Understanding the internal representations of deep neural networks remains one of the central challenges in machine learning research. This project explores concept-based interpretability methods — specifically Testing with Concept Activation Vectors (TCAV) and its visual extension Visual-TCAV — to quantify how specific human-interpretable concepts influence model predictions.

Through systematic experimentation and analysis, we evaluate the sensitivity, specificity, and reliability of concept attribution across diverse model architectures and datasets. The findings presented here contribute to the broader goal of making neural network decisions more transparent, auditable, and trustworthy.
