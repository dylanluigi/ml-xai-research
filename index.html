Opening the Black Box of Neural Networks

This work investigates concept-based explainability as a means of resolving the tension between predictive capability and accountability, treating an explanation as a mapping from a model’s activation space onto a vocabulary of human-understandable concepts.

Method

Two complementary phases

A controlled phase checked against a closed-form ground truth, and a clinical phase checked against the judgement of a practising dermatologist.

01

Controlled validation

The composition of the negative pool, not the construction of the concept direction, governs faithfulness. Only a matched, concept-absent pool recovers the correct attribution, and the signal concentrates at the bottleneck layer.

02

Clinical transfer

Malignant concepts drive the melanoma decision and benign regular patterns drive the nevus decision, inverting with the target class. Seborrheic keratosis exposes a vocabulary gap rather than a model deficit.

03

Corroboration

The recovered concepts are confirmed by the standard TCAV sign counts and by an independent expert dermatologist, who agrees with the model’s concept calls on close to four fifths of the comparisons.
