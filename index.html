Research methodology

Concept definition

Curate positive and negative concept examples from standardised vision datasets with controlled confounding factors.

CAV training

Train binary linear classifiers in activation space at multiple network depths to capture hierarchical concept encoding.

Attribution scoring

Compute directional derivatives and aggregate TCAV scores with bootstrapped confidence intervals across random seeds.
